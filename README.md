# Daybook Planner

An all-in-one digital planner built to replace a paper agenda on a phone and Android tablet.

## What's inside

- **Home**: a dashboard with what's up next, today's top 3, year progress, family, goals, habits, budget, debt payoff and workout charts
- **Year, Month, Week and Day** pages with US holidays, stickers, an hourly schedule, to-dos and notes
- **Goals & Habits**: six yearly goals with progress, plus a monthly habit tracker
- **Budget**: planned vs. actual spending, income and debt payoff
- **Fitness**: a workout log with monthly totals and charts
- **Family**: each person's activities, a weekly meal plan, a grocery list and weekly to-dos
- **Work Notes**: meeting, project and brain-dump notes
- **Customize**: light and dark styles, covers and the planner's name

## Testing

Open the live site (GitHub Pages) on your phone, tablet or computer:

**https://xca1b3r.github.io/daybook-planner/**

To install it on Android, open the site in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then **Add to Home screen**.

### Running outside Claude

This version is the same planner published in Claude. Outside Claude:

- Entries save in that browser on that device only, and don't sync between devices.
- Google Calendar shows a message instead of your events, because the current connection goes through Claude.

A standalone Google sign-in for Calendar and syncing is planned as the next step.
